Frequently asked questions

Will Easeva take over talking to my clients?+

No — you're always in control Easeva drafts replies, quotes, and forms, but nothing goes to a client until you've approved it

What if I don't want to hand everything over straight away?+

You don't have to Start with the bits you're comfortable with — like call answering — and hand over more as you get confident

Do I need to change my number or how clients reach me?+

For calls, it's still your number — nothing changes there For messaging, it depends on how deep you want to go: if you've already got WhatsApp Business, we can connect straight to that If not, we can set you up with a new number just for that

What does it actually do for me?+

It picks up calls when you're not free, drafts quotes and replies, keeps track of your jobs, fills out paperwork and certificates from what you tell it, and chases things up so you don't have to

Is my client and business data safe?+

Yes — your data is kept secure and private, and it's never shared or used for anything beyond running your business

What will it cost?+

Which trades is this for?+

Any sole trader who deals with enquiries, quotes, and paperwork — plumbers, electricians, heating engineers, decorators, and more

We're onboarding waitlist members first, in order — the sooner you join, the sooner you're in
